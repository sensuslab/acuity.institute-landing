About the Acuity Institute

The Acuity Institute is an independent project dedicated to truth and the expansion of accurate knowledge, enlightening pathways of understanding across all areas of life. It offers pathways to wisdom through library, philosophy and webinars.

Founder

The Institute was founded, and is stewarded, by Ethan James Farrell.

The Archive offers an open reading collection of PDF and EPUB editions. Source details and historical context accompany each text. A future workspace for AI-assisted inquiry is in development.
