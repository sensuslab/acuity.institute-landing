Privacy Policy

Effective from 11 November 2026

This policy covers the Acuity Institute website, the public Acuity Library, and the contact and waitlist forms on the website.

Acuity.Codex, Acuity.Search and Acuity.Agent are forthcoming products. Each app will have its own privacy policy available from its product page at launch. The Acuity.Agent waitlist form on this website is covered by this website policy.

Who is responsible for your data

Ethan James Farrell operates Acuity Institute in the United Kingdom and is the data controller for this website. For privacy questions, requests or complaints, email data@acuity.institute.

What we collect and why

Contact form

We collect your name, email address and message so we can reply and follow up on your enquiry. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in handling correspondence. If you ask us to take specific steps before entering into a contract with you, we process the information needed to take those steps.

Acuity.Agent waitlist

We collect your name and email address to register your interest and contact you when early access becomes available. You can also tell us what you would like to explore so we can understand your interests. We use this information with your consent when you join the waitlist.

The optional checkbox for occasional Acuity Institute updates records a separate choice. We use your email address to send those updates only if you tick it. You can leave the waitlist or stop updates at any time by emailing data@acuity.institute.

Information sent with either form

Both forms send a randomly generated submission reference, the submission type, the time of submission and the site address. We use this information to route submissions, keep accurate records and avoid duplicate entries when a submission is retried. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in operating the forms reliably.

The contact form requires a name, email address and message. The waitlist requires a name and email address. The waitlist note and updates checkbox are optional. Please keep messages and notes relevant and avoid including sensitive personal information, such as details about your health or religious beliefs.

Website and Library use

When your browser requests a page or file, hosting and content delivery providers process technical information such as your IP address, browser information, the URL requested and the time of the request. We use this to deliver the website and its files, protect the service and diagnose problems. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in providing a secure and reliable website. The public Library is available without an account.

Who handles the information

  • Railway hosts the website and our self-hosted n8n service in Amsterdam, the Netherlands. The n8n service receives and routes form submissions. We use a private Notion database to manage form submissions and an email provider to send replies, early-access messages and requested updates.
  • Cloudflare helps deliver and protect site content, including Library files. Its network processes the technical information needed to serve those requests and may use cookies for security.
  • Google Fonts serves some site typefaces. Your browser sends Google technical request data, including your IP address, when it loads them.
  • YouTube serves previews for some webinar videos and loads its player if you choose to play one. Requests to YouTube share technical information with Google. The player may also use cookies or similar storage. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it. Our lawful basis in those cases is compliance with the legal obligation.

International transfers

The website and form service are hosted in the Netherlands. The UK recognises the European Economic Area as providing adequate protection for personal data. Some providers, including Notion and Cloudflare, operate internationally and may process data in the United States or other countries. Where a transfer needs additional protection under UK law, the safeguards depend on the provider and destination and can include standard contractual clauses with the UK International Data Transfer Addendum. Email data@acuity.institute for details of the safeguards that apply or to request a copy.

How long we keep information

  • Enquiries: for as long as needed to handle the enquiry and any follow-up. We may keep records longer where needed for a legal obligation or to deal with a dispute.
  • Waitlist: while you are waiting for early access and for as long as needed to complete the related invitation process. You can ask to leave earlier.
  • Optional updates: while you remain subscribed. We may keep a record of your choice where needed to honour an opt-out or demonstrate consent.
  • Technical records: for as long as needed to operate and secure the website or investigate an issue, subject to the provider’s retention settings.

Information stored in your browser

The e-book reader can save your font size and place in a book when you choose “Remember my place on this device”. Turning that option off removes the saved position and font size for the book. Changing the page colour saves that preference in your browser. You can remove saved reading preferences by clearing the site data in your browser.

Each form keeps a temporary submission reference in your current browser tab so it can reuse the same reference if you retry. The reference is sent with the form and removed from browser storage after a successful submission or when the browser session for that tab ends.

Your rights and complaints

Under UK data protection law, you can ask to access or correct your personal data. Depending on the circumstances, you can also ask for erasure, restriction or a portable copy, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can withdraw consent at any time. This does not affect processing carried out before you withdrew it.

Email data@acuity.institute to exercise your rights or raise a data protection complaint. We will acknowledge complaints within 30 days and tell you the outcome of our review. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).
