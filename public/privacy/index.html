2. Summary

  • You can read and download everything in the Library without an account, and we do not identify you while you read.

  • We only receive personal data that you choose to send us through a form.
  • The e-book reader can remember your place, but only in your own browser. That information never leaves your device.

3.3 Technical data when you visit

When your browser requests a page, our hosting provider processes your IP address, browser type, the address requested and the time of the request, so it can deliver the site and keep it secure. We do not use this data to profile you.

Lawful basis: legitimate interests (operating and securing the website).

4. Data stored on your own device

The site does not use cookies. It does use your browser's own storage for the following purposes. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else.

Storage Key What is stored When How long
Local storage acuity.ebook.v1.<book-id>.<edition> Your chosen font size and your position in that e-book (an EPUB location reference) Only if you tick "Remember my place on this device" in the e-book reader Until you untick the option (which deletes it) or clear your browser's site data
Local storage acuity.ebook.appearance.v1 Your chosen e-book page colour (White, Soft white or Warm) When you change the page colour Until you clear your browser's site data
Session storage acuity-contact-submission-id, acuity-agent-waitlist-submission-id A random reference number for a form you are filling in, so that a retried submission isn't recorded twice When you send a form Removed after a successful send, or when you close the tab

The PDF reader doesn't store anything on your device. Because these items are strictly necessary for features you request, or are set only at your explicit choice, no cookie consent banner is required. [LEGAL REVIEW: confirm under PECR reg. 6]

To clear them: untick "Remember my place on this device" in the reader, or delete this site's data in your browser settings (for example, Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Site settings → View permissions and data stored across sites → acuity.institute → Delete data).
