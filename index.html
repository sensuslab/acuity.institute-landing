The Acuity Library: a library for the enquiring mind

The Library is an open reading room for the world's wisdom traditions: 282 texts and presentations across Esoteric, Philosophy, Scripture, Presentations, Original Works, Health and Miscellaneous collections. Every text is free to read and download as PDF, with EPUB editions for many. No account is needed. Enter the library.

Acuity.Agent: Creating Pathways to Wisdom

Acuity.Agent is the Acuity Institute's knowledge assistant for exploring sacred texts, philosophy and spiritual traditions. It searches a library of more than 20,000 sacred texts, reads the relevant passages and develops answers with traceable source references. Acuity.Agent is pre-release; access is by waitlist.

How the research works

1. Search: locates relevant material in the knowledge library. 2. Read: inspects passages and surrounding context. 3. Synthesise: connects evidence to the question and cites sources. 4. Clarify limits: distinguishes quotation from interpretation and states when evidence cannot be found.

Instruments of Clarity (under development)

A single future interface for self-exploration and creation, building on the Library and Acuity.Agent. Its facets: The Archive (discover and read the curated corpus), The Lens (ask questions, compare sources, inspect evidence), myVault (personal workspaces, notes, conversations and outputs), Context Spheres (visually organise and explore a workspace) and Studio (summaries, study material and audio from selected sources). None is available yet.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Acuity Institute?

The Acuity Institute is an independent project, founded by Ethan James Farrell, dedicated to truth and the expansion of accurate knowledge. Its first offering is the Acuity Library, an open reading library of wisdom traditions.

Is the Acuity Library free to use?

Yes. Every text in the Library is free to read in the browser and to download. No account is needed and nothing is tracked.

What does the Library contain?

The Library holds 282 texts and presentations across seven collections: Esoteric, Philosophy, Scripture, Presentations, Original Works, Health and Miscellaneous. Texts are available as PDF, with EPUB editions for many and plain text for some.

What is Acuity.Agent?

Acuity.Agent is the Institute's AI knowledge assistant for exploring sacred texts, philosophy and spiritual traditions. It searches a library of more than 20,000 sacred texts, reads the relevant passages and develops answers with traceable source references. It is not yet open to the public; you can join the waitlist on this page.

Are the Instruments of Clarity available yet?

No. The Instruments of Clarity are under development. They are planned as a single interface, with The Archive, The Lens, myVault, Context Spheres and Studio as its facets, building on the Library and Acuity.Agent.

Does the e-book reader remember my place?

Only if you choose "Remember my place on this device". Your font size and position in the book are then saved in your own browser and are not sent to the Institute. Unticking the option removes the saved entry.
