Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026

This policy explains how personal data is handled when you visit acuity.institute (the Acuity Institute) or contact us through it.

1. Who we are

The data controller is Ethan James Farrell, based in the United Kingdom, who operates the Acuity Institute ("we", "us"). We handle personal data under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. Because some processing takes place in the Netherlands, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR) may also apply.

Contact for privacy matters: hello@acuity.institute

2. What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address and message.
  • Technical data: when you load the site, our hosting and network providers automatically process your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the time of the request. This is used to deliver the site, keep it secure and diagnose faults.
  • We do not knowingly collect special category data. Please do not include sensitive information, such as health or religious details, in your message unless you want us to use it to respond.

3. Why we use it, and our lawful basis

Purpose Lawful basis
Replying to your enquiry and any follow-up conversation Legitimate interests (responding to people who contact us). Where your enquiry is about working together, steps taken at your request before entering into a contract
Running, securing and troubleshooting the website Legitimate interests (keeping the site available and safe)
Keeping records where we are legally required to Legal obligation

Contact-form submissions are sent to our self-hosted n8n automation server in Amsterdam, Netherlands, which passes them on to us, and they are then handled in the United Kingdom.

6. International transfers

Your enquiry is handled in the United Kingdom and processed on our server in the Netherlands. The UK recognises the EEA as providing adequate protection, so transfers between the UK and the Netherlands do not need extra safeguards.

Some providers (Cloudflare, Google and Railway) are based in the United States or operate globally. Where data goes to the US, we rely on the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or on the International Data Transfer Addendum or Agreement approved by the ICO. [CONFIRM: check each provider's current certification or data processing agreement, particularly Railway's.]

8. Your rights

Under UK GDPR (and EU GDPR where it applies), you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you
  • have inaccurate data corrected
  • have your data erased
  • restrict or object to our processing
  • data portability, where it applies

To use any of these rights, email hello@acuity.institute. We will respond within one month. There is normally no charge.

9. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, telephone 0303 123 1113. If you live in the EU, you may complain to your local data protection authority.
